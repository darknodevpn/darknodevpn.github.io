Capabilities

Built for a quieter connection

Everything in Darknode exists to open an encrypted tunnel, pick a working server, and stay honest about data.

Encrypted tunnel

Device traffic is routed through an encrypted VPN tunnel to a remote server endpoint. That is the core function of the app.

System-wide VPN

Darknode uses Android’s VpnService, so the tunnel covers the whole device — not just a single browser.

Smart server pick

The app fetches the active server list, probes latency, and connects to a working endpoint. If one fails, it tries another.

One-tap connect

No account, no setup. Review the disclosure, tap START, and Darknode opens the tunnel.

No traffic contents

We do not collect browsing history, messages, passwords, or the contents of your encrypted VPN traffic.

Disclosure first

Before you connect, Darknode shows a VPN data disclosure. You can decline and not use the VPN features.

How it works

Start. Tunnel. Done.

The in-app flow is deliberately short: review, tap START, wait for Secured.

01

02

03

Secured

Privacy

What we access — and what we don’t

Darknode collects only what it needs to run the VPN. The full policy lives at darknodevpn.com/privacy-policy.

We may process

  • A hashed device identifier for service operation
  • Approximate country and public IP before you connect

We do not collect

  • Browsing history
  • Messages, passwords, or traffic contents
  • DNS query contents for profiling
  • Personal profiles for sale — we do not sell your information
