VPN data disclosure

This matches the in-app disclosure shown before Darknode can connect.

Why this app uses VPN

Darknode creates an encrypted VPN tunnel to route your device traffic through our remote servers. This is the core function of the app and requires Android's VpnService permission.

Data accessed or collected

• Device identifier (hashed) for quota management
• Approximate location (country) and IP address before VPN connection
• VPN session traffic volume (upload/download bytes)
• Rewarded ad interaction counts

We do not collect browsing history, messages, or the contents of your traffic.

How data is used and shared

Collected data is used to enforce data limits, sync your allowance across reinstalls, and operate the service. It is transmitted to our backend (Cloudflare). Traffic is encrypted between your device and the VPN server endpoint.

Advertising

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) let you reset or extend your data allowance. While the VPN is connected, ad requests leave through the VPN exit so they can reach Google's servers. We do not redirect or manipulate ad traffic from other apps.
