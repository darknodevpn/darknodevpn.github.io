Darknode VPN Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Darknode (“we”, “us”, or “the app”) is a VPN client for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access or collect, why we need it, and how it is used when you use Darknode VPN.

1. Who we are

Darknode provides encrypted VPN connectivity so your device traffic can be routed through remote VPN servers. The app uses Android’s VpnService API for this core function.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Device identifier (hashed) — a one-way hash used only to manage your data quota across reinstalls.
  • Network metadata before VPN connect — approximate country and public IP address of your real connection, used for service operation and abuse prevention.
  • VPN session traffic volume — upload/download byte counts to enforce the data allowance.
  • Rewarded ad interaction counts — to apply quota resets/bonuses and enforce daily ad limits.

We do not collect browsing history, DNS query contents for profiling, messages, passwords, or the contents of your encrypted VPN traffic.

3. How we use information

  • Operate the VPN service and maintain a working connection
  • Enforce data quotas and sync allowance across reinstalls
  • Show optional rewarded ads that can extend your data allowance
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse

4. VPN data practices

When connected, device traffic is routed through an encrypted tunnel to our configured VPN server endpoint. Traffic between your device and that endpoint is encrypted. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising

Darknode may show optional Google AdMob rewarded ads. Ads are loaded by the app process outside the VPN tunnel and are not used to redirect or manipulate advertising traffic from other apps. Google may process device advertising identifiers and related signals under Google’s policies. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), we present a consent form via Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are requested.

6. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare — backend storage/API for quota/profile fields and VPN server list
  • Google AdMob / UMP — rewarded ads and privacy messaging
  • VPN server operators — to provide the tunnel endpoint you connect to

7. Data retention

Quota and profile fields are retained while needed to operate the service and enforce limits. You may request deletion of your hashed device record by contacting us.

8. Children’s privacy

Darknode is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices

  • You can decline the in-app VPN disclosure and not use the VPN features.
  • You can refuse optional rewarded ads; core VPN use does not require watching ads.
  • Where shown, you can manage privacy choices through the consent / privacy options UI.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us at darknodevpn.com or email the address listed on the Google Play store listing.
